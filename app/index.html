Roadmap to the platinum

Around 200 hours, most of it the 100% checklist. There are no online trophies, no difficulty trophies and no mission medals — it is long, not hard. One rule matters more than any other: there is no mission replay and no New Game Plus, so keep a manual save at the start of every chapter and one untouched save from just before the final mission.

How this guide is built

Trophy names, descriptions and grades are the official PlayStation 5 list as published on 18 November 2026 (Exophase, as reported by Push Square). Each guide is written for this page in its own words and names its sources beside it; where no unlock method was confirmed at launch, the guide says so instead of guessing. The five trophies with no confirmed method at launch are In The Beginning…, Capital Offense, Bad Luck, I Guess, The Truth Is Out There and Ten Pin.

Missable flags come from the same sources: the only confirmed missable risk is Lucia’s balcony meditation in Chapter 1, which counts toward Positive Mental Attitude, plus the time-sensitive Race Against Yago in Chapter 3, which counts toward Strangers & Freaks. Everything else stays available after the credits.
